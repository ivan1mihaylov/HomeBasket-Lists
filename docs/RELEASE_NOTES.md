**What you configure here is kept by HomeBasket too.**

HomeBasket owns the products, so a list should not be the only place that knows
one. Anything to buy that is added or configured on a list — its name, its
picture, the kind of shop it comes from — is now handed to HomeBasket as a
product without a barcode. From then on it is offered by name as you type,
anywhere, with everything it already knows; and in the HomeBasket card you can
give it a barcode, after which scanning finds this same product.

Tasks stay here — they are the list's own business, not products.

**The dropdowns follow the theme.** The list a select drops down, and the
calendar a date field opens, are drawn by the browser and were coming up white
on a dark dashboard. They now follow the colour scheme the theme is in.

Needs HomeBasket 0.11.0 to keep what is configured.
