A product that Open Food Facts says is sold in a shop you have configured for
the list now arrives with that shop already set. Only your own zones are
considered, so a product sold in fifty chains does not pick one at random, and
a shop you set yourself always wins.

hard against the right edge, and the quantity field hung a few pixels above the
buttons beside it. Both are straight now, and the product at the top of the
sheet no longer explains that it can be tapped — the outline and the arrow
already say so.
