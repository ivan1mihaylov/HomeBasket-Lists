Quantities are now said rather than read out.

A list is written short and speech is not: an item written *яйца, 5 бр.* is
spoken as *5 броя яйца* — how many first, the way it is said out loud — and *1
бр.* as *1 брой*. кг, г, л and мл are said in full too, and a decimal is said
with a comma: *1,5 килограма*.

A unit the answer's language does not know is said exactly as it was typed, and
English keeps its own order, with a dash: *eggs - 5 pcs*.
