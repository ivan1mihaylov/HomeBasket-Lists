**An eighth kind of shop: medicines.** On an item and in the list's settings,
where it takes the zones it is worth a reminder in like every other.

**Groceries no longer carry a best-before date.** This is a list of what to
buy, not a record of what is in the cupboard, so the field is gone from the
item sheet and the date no longer shows on the row. Nothing else about a
grocery changes; a task still has its deadline, and a product still says where
it comes from.

Best with HomeBasket 0.11.2, which is where a product's category comes from.
